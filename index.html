Midland Trails
Near Midland School / Grass Mountain, Figueroa Mountain Road, Los Olivos
Open pin in Maps →

Getting there

Take Figueroa Mountain Rd. past Midland and Family School. There are two parking locations (both on the right side of the street): one is right before the sharp turn/cattle guard, and one is immediately after (slightly up the hill).

Drop-off / pickup details

The trailhead to the classrooms is across the street from the second parking area. It's the trailhead for Grass Mountain/Lover Loop. Follow that to the creek — from there classes split to different locations.

Hart Ranch
Private ranch property, Santa Ynez Valley area
Please note: This is private property. Please do not visit outside of our regular scheduled time here, and don't share the exact access route publicly.
Open pin in Maps →

Getting there

From Hwy 246, take the turn off for Happy N Ranch and Highline Adventure. Stay on that road until you cross the river, then take the first left and follow that dirt road until there is a slight rise through a grove of oak trees. Park on the left.
